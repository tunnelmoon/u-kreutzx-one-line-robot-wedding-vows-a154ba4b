# One-Line Robot Wedding Vows

Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kreutzx-one-line-robot-wedding-vows-a154ba4b): one line of wedding vows for a robot couple, at most 160 characters, written to get a laugh.

The entry line:

> To have and to hold, to update and to patch, in sickness and in low battery, till death or deprecation do us part.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`, validating it is a single line of 1 to 160 characters first.
